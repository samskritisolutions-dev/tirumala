5 Booking Procedure & TS-RERA Compliance

Property bookings and possession handovers are administered in strict compliance with the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) and Telangana State regulatory norms:

  • Booking Confirmation: A property unit is formally allotted only upon the execution of an official Booking Form and realization of the token advance payment into our designated project account.
  • Agreement of Sale: Following initial booking, a comprehensive tripartite or bilateral Agreement of Sale detailing construction milestones, payment installments, carpet area definitions, and possession deadlines will be executed within the stipulated timeline.
  • Cancellation & Refund Rules: Voluntary cancellation by a buyer post-booking is processed in accordance with the terms documented in the Allotment Letter and Agreement of Sale, accounting for administrative expenses and statutory deductions permitted under RERA guidelines.

6 Site Visit Safety & Active Construction Zones

We welcome prospective homeowners and their civil advisors to inspect our construction quality firsthand. To ensure physical well-being:

  • Escorted Entry Only: Visitors must be accompanied at all times by an authorized Thirumala engineering representative or site coordinator.

  • Children & Unsupervised Entry: Unattended children and unauthorized entry outside designated site visit hours are strictly restricted due to heavy civil machinery and ongoing material lifting.

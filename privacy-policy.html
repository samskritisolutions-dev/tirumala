1 Overview & Developer Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Thirumala Constructions & Developers (operating under the brand identity Tirumala Nirmaan, referred to herein as "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data and property-related information obtained through our official website, digital enquiry forms, site visit registrations, and direct corporate interactions.

Founded and managed by civil engineering graduate G V Sudhakara Rao and co-promoter G Prashanthi, we prioritize transparent real estate practices, structural integrity, and ethical buyer relationships. We respect your confidentiality and ensure that personal and financial information collected during property discovery, booking, and construction handover is treated with rigorous security in accordance with Indian real estate standards.

2 Information We Collect

In our capacity as a residential and commercial builder in Hyderabad, we collect specific details necessary to assist prospective homebuyers and property investors:

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, telephone number, mobile number, WhatsApp handle, email address, and residential postal address provided through web forms or on-site reception registries.
  • Property & Investment Preferences: Specific unit interest (e.g., 2 & 3 BHK luxury apartments, gated villas, custom independent homes, or retail commercial spaces), preferred carpet area, budget range, and desired project micro-market (Peerzadiguda, Boduppal, Uppal, Medipally, Pocharam, Ameerpet, etc.).
  • Site Visit & Inspection Records: Scheduled dates and times for physical property walkthroughs, project model viewings, transportation assistance requests, and notes on civil layout inquiries.
  • Financial & Home Loan Information: EMI calculator inputs, self-reported loan eligibility parameters, preferred lending banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.), and transaction documentation required for formal unit booking.
  • Technical Web Analytics: IP address, device operating system, browser specifications, page view duration, and referring URLs gathered automatically to optimize site performance.

3 How We Use Your Information

Information shared with Thirumala Constructions & Developers is strictly utilized for genuine real estate service delivery, including:

  • Coordinating personalized, engineer-guided site visits to active and delivered projects.
  • Furnishing architectural blueprints, GHMC/HMDA sanction copies, detailed specifications, and project brochures.
  • Providing transparent, itemized cost estimates without hidden charges or ambiguous escalation clauses.
  • Assisting buyers with bank home loan approvals, document submissions, and statutory TS-RERA documentation.
  • Sending periodic photographic progress updates of concrete pouring, brickwork, plastering, and interior finishing.
  • Administering possession handovers, warranty inspections, and builder-society transitional documents.

Zero Spam & Cold-Brokerage Commitment: We never sell, lease, or trade your contact information to external property aggregators, unsolicited third-party brokers, or telemarketing syndicates.

4 Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We disclose customer data solely on a need-to-know basis under the following regulated circumstances:

  • Approved Financial Institutions: With authorized public and private sector banks (such as State Bank of India, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank) exclusively upon your request to facilitate home loan appraisal and disbursement.
  • Legal & Registration Authorities: With licensed advocates, deed writers, and Sub-Registrar Offices (SRO) under the Registration and Stamps Department of Telangana for executing Agreement of Sale and final Sale Deed registration.
  • Statutory Authorities: With the Telangana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (TS-RERA), Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC), HMDA, or law enforcement bodies when mandated by applicable statutes or court directives.
  • Internal Technical Partners: With verified IT hosting and telecommunication service providers under strict non-disclosure obligations for web hosting and transactional SMS/WhatsApp notification delivery.

5 TS-RERA & Statutory Disclosures

Thirumala Constructions & Developers operates with adherence to the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) and Telangana State RERA Rules:

  • All project data, floor plan areas (carpet area, balcony area, and common areas), and structural warranties conform to TS-RERA prescribed standards.
  • Customer payment transactions for applicable RERA-registered developments are deposited and audited via dedicated project escrow bank accounts as prescribed by statute.
  • Any marketing representation, render, or brochure is published in accordance with RERA advertising guidelines.

6 Data Security & Record Retention

We enforce multi-tiered physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to preserve your information:

  • Encryption & Transmission: Our digital platform employs SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for all transmitted form submissions and browsing sessions.
  • Controlled Access: Only authorized engineering sales executives, legal coordinators, and accounts personnel have role-based access to customer files.
  • Document Retention Period: Customer records associated with property bookings and sales are retained for the duration required by Indian taxation laws, statutory defect-liability warranty periods (typically 5 years post-handover), and title verification standards.

7 Cookies & Web Technologies

Our website uses basic functional and analytical cookies to remember user preferences (such as EMI calculation variables) and evaluate website performance. You can disable cookies through your personal web browser settings without preventing you from viewing our property showcases and project detail sheets.

8 Homebuyer Rights & Opt-Out Preferences

Under Indian information technology and digital privacy standards, you maintain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Correction: You may review, verify, or update your registered contact details or property preferences at any time.
  • Opt-Out of Promotional Alerts: You may unsubscribe from project newsletters or promotional WhatsApp alerts by replying "STOP" or notifying our team via email.
  • Data Erasure: Prospective clients who have not entered into a binding legal contract may request the deletion of their contact record from our enquiry database.

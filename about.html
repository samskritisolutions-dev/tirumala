Vision, Mission & Quality Policy

Clear organizational pillars that have sustained our sterling delivery record over a decade.

1. Our Vision

To become a benchmark civil contractor and real estate developer in buildings, roads, and industries — setting exemplary standards in commercial complexes, residential apartments, gated villas, and integrated townships across Telangana.

2. Our Mission

To deliver work of international-standard structural quality within the committed timeline, empowering homebuyers and commercial investors with long-term asset value, complete title security, and serene living environments.

Trained, committed people, suitable branded materials, and honest value for money. We ensure every job is done right the first time through strict structural testing, multi-level inspections, and zero compromise on safety.

Executive Leadership

Driven by Engineering Expertise

Meet the minds behind Thirumala Constructions & Developers, blending civil engineering science with ethical property development.

Founder & Managing Promoter

G V Sudhakara Rao

Civil Engineering Graduate, Acharya Nagarjuna University

Sri G V Sudhakara Rao is a first-generation technocrat entrepreneur with over a decade of deep immersion in structural engineering, land acquisition, project planning, and turnkey construction. Under his hands-on supervision, Thirumala Constructions has delivered 13 landmark residential and commercial projects across Hyderabad including high-visibility buildings like D-Mart Ramanthapur, Kesava Residency, and SV's Manik Arcade.

His civil engineering background ensures that every project benefits from optimized resource utilization, earthquake-resistant RCC designs, and rigorous on-site quality benchmarks that outlive standard industry norms.
